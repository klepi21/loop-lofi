HOW IT WORKS ON SUI

A yeti that remembers every bonk

LOOP uses Sui so your yeti and your best moments are really yours. No seed phrases, no gas to buy. Built for the hackathon and running on Sui devnet.

Live on Sui devnet

Sign in with Google

zkLogin turns your Google account into a Sui wallet. No seed phrase to write down, no extension to install.

Sponsored gas

Every transaction is paid for by the game. You never need SUI to play.

Free tournaments

Free entry. The prize pot is sponsored and held by a Move contract, which pays the winners when the bracket ends.

Yeti NFTs with their own history

Your yeti is an NFT that carries its own kills, wins and level. After each match the server signs the result and the stats are written on chain. Trade your yeti through Kiosk and its record goes with it.

Clip NFTs on Walrus

Your best kill clip is stored on Walrus and minted as a Clip NFT you can keep or show off.

QUESTIONS

Before you throw

Is LOOP free?

Yes. LOOP is free to play and you never need SUI to play. Gas is sponsored.

Can I play on my phone?

Not yet. LOOP is built for a desktop browser with keyboard and mouse. Open this page on your computer and press play.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. You sign in with Google and zkLogin gives you a Sui address behind the scenes. No seed phrase, no extension.

Is this on Sui mainnet?

No. For the CLAY hackathon everything runs on Sui devnet. Yeti NFTs, Clip NFTs and tournament pots are devnet assets with no real money value.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Make a room, share the four letter code and your friends jump straight in. Bots fill any seats that are still empty.

What happens when I bonk someone?

The server signs the match result and your Yeti NFT records it on chain. Kills, wins and level stay with that yeti, even if you trade it.
