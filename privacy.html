LOOP privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026. LOOP (Lofi Out Of Patience) is a free browser game made for the CLAY hackathon on Sui devnet.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, Google gives the game a signed token with your account id and first name. The LOOP server uses it once to create a stable zkLogin salt for your Sui address. We do not store your email or your Google token.

When you play online, the server sees the name shown in the match and your match results. If a Sui wallet is connected, results are stored with your wallet address so they can be written to your Yeti NFT and counted for tournaments.

On chain data

Yeti NFTs, Clip NFTs, tournament entries and payouts live on the public Sui devnet. Clips you choose to store go to Walrus testnet, which is public storage. Do not upload anything you want to keep private.

Cookies and storage

The game keeps your settings, progress and an optional guest wallet key in your browser storage. There are no ads and no tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/klepi21/loop-lofi.
