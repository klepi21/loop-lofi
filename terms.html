LOOP terms

Last updated 18 November 2026.

The game

LOOP is a free game made for a hackathon. It is offered as is, it can change or stop at any time, and online play may be reset.

Devnet only

Every Sui item in LOOP lives on Sui devnet: Yeti NFTs, Clip NFTs, tournament pots and payouts. Devnet assets have no money value and devnet is wiped from time to time, which removes them.

Fair play

Be kind. No cheating, no offensive names, no abuse of the sponsored gas. We can block wallets or players who do.
